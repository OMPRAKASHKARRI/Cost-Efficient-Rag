Frequently Asked Questions

What are your support hours?

Standard support is available Monday through Friday, 9 AM to 5 PM Eastern Time. Enterprise customers receive 24/7 phone support through their dedicated success manager.

Is Synergy Labs SOC 2 compliant?

Yes. Synergy Labs completed its SOC 2 Type II audit in March 2025 and undergoes an annual re-certification. Compliance reports are available to Enterprise customers under NDA upon request.

What are the API rate limits?

The default rate limit is 60 requests per minute per API key. Enterprise customers can request a higher limit of up to 600 requests per minute by contacting their account manager.

How is customer data encrypted?

All data is encrypted at rest using AES-256 and in transit using TLS 1.3. Encryption keys are rotated automatically every 90 days.

Do you offer a data export tool?

Yes. Customers on any plan can export their full dataset as a CSV or JSON archive from the account settings page at any time, with no additional fee.
