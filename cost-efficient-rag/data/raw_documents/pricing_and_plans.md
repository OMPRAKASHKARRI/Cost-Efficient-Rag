# Synergy Labs Pricing and Plans

## Free Plan

The Free plan is limited to 1,000 API calls per month and includes access to a single project workspace. It does not include priority support or SSO integration. Free plan data is retained for 7 days before automatic deletion.

## Pro Plan

The Pro plan costs $49 per month per seat and includes 100,000 API calls per month, five project workspaces, and email support with a 24-hour response time. Pro plan data is retained for 90 days.

## Enterprise Plan

The Enterprise plan is custom-priced and includes unlimited API calls, unlimited workspaces, SSO via SAML, a dedicated customer success manager, and a 99.9% uptime SLA. Enterprise data retention is configurable up to 7 years.

## Billing Cycle

All plans are billed monthly in advance. Annual billing is available on the Pro and Enterprise plans with a 15% discount applied to the total contract value.

## Cancellation Policy

Customers may cancel at any time from the billing settings page. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period, and no partial refunds are issued for the remaining days in that period.

## Payment Methods

Synergy Labs accepts Visa, Mastercard, and American Express for monthly billing. Enterprise customers may alternatively pay by wire transfer against a quarterly invoice.
